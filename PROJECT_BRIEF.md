# Premier Coaching and Training Center

Premier Coaching and Training Center will provide practical computer training, academic coaching, and English-language preparation.

## Computer Training

- Microsoft Office: Word, Excel, and PowerPoint
- Adobe Photoshop and basic graphic design
- Digital marketing
- Internet, email, and basic computer skills
- Other beginner-friendly courses

## Academic Coaching

- Classes 4 and 5 through Intermediate/HSC level
- General academic support
- Special focus on science subjects
- Exam preparation and regular assessments

## English and IELTS

- Spoken and basic English
- IELTS preparation
- Reading, writing, listening, and speaking practice
